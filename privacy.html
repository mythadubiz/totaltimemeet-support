Privacy Policy

🔒 Your Privacy Matters

Total Time Meet is designed with privacy at its core. We believe your data belongs to you and only you.

No Data Collection

Total Time Meet does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. All your preferences (timer presets, alarm sound selection, blink warning threshold) are stored locally on your device only using standard iOS storage.

No Analytics

We do not use any analytics frameworks, crash reporting tools, or tracking SDKs. We have no way to know how you use the app.

No Third-Party Services

Total Time Meet does not integrate with any third-party services. The only external communication is with Apple's servers for processing in-app purchases through StoreKit, which is handled entirely by Apple.

In-App Purchases

Total Time Meet offers a one-time premium purchase processed securely through Apple's App Store. We do not have access to your payment information. All transaction handling is managed by Apple.

Audio & Microphone

Total Time Meet uses the audio session to play alarm sounds through the speaker, even when the device is on silent mode. The app does not record audio or access the microphone for any purpose. No audio data is collected or transmitted.

Children's Privacy

Total Time Meet does not collect any data from any user, including children. The app is safe for all ages.

Changes to This Policy

If we ever update this privacy policy, changes will be reflected on this page. Given that we collect no data, we do not anticipate any significant changes.

Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please contact us at mythadubiz@gmail.com.
