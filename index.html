❓ FAQ

How do I restore my purchase?
Open Settings (gear icon) → tap "Unlock Premium" → tap "Restore Purchases". Your premium access will be restored instantly.


How do I add a custom timer preset?
Open Settings → scroll to "Add Preset" → use the wheel pickers to set hours, minutes, and seconds → tap "Add Preset".


How do I change the alarm sound?
Open Settings → scroll to "Alarm Sound" → select from the available sounds. Free users get the voice alert. Premium unlocks all 30 sounds.


Does the alarm work on silent mode?
Yes! The alarm plays through the speaker even when your iPhone is on silent or vibrate mode.


What is the blink warning?
With Premium, you can set a percentage threshold. When remaining time drops below that threshold, the display blinks to grab attention.


Is my data collected?
No. Total Time Meet does not collect any personal data. Everything is stored locally on your device.
